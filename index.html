02 / ABOUT

Curiosity with a practical side.

I'm Adam, a Spokane-based IT project management professional exploring photography, creative tools, and the systems that help organize digital work.

My background includes coordinating technology projects, writing customer-facing documentation, and building relationships through community volunteer work. Outside of that, I'm developing my photography practice and experimenting with digital asset management.
